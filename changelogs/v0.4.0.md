## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- **Unified VikingHub Integration**: Added a dedicated **Climate** tab inside the centralized VikingHub window displaying live thermal status, shelter quality, and active weather effects.
- **Dissimilar Material Joint Resistance**: Added a configurable thermal contact barrier (default 95% reduction) across joints between dissimilar materials (e.g. wood to stone), allowing interior wood lining to insulate inside stone shells.
- **Dwelling-Wide Comfort Queries**: Extended thermal dwelling comfort evaluation to position-based comfort queries for seamless compatibility with hospitality and base-scanning mods.

### Changed
- **Thermal Solver & Ambient Tuning**: Refined ambient temperature calculations, thermal relaxation solver behavior, and gear insulation handling, and updated integration for VikingLib v0.4.0.
